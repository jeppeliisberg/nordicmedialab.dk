Federating Nordic Media Lab

Workshop 1

Needs and principles

DateTuesday 18 August 2026
Time16.00–19.00
PlaceDemokrati Lab, Rentemestervej
ModeratorBjarke Winding, chairperson of Nordic Media Lab

RSVP Email bjarke@nordicmedialab.dk if you would like to join.

We begin the work of defining a shared platform for our different social platforms. To build broad understanding and common ground, we start the series with the overall needs and principles for a shared infrastructure and its protocols, before we look at concrete solutions.

How this runs depends on how many we are. Up to about ten, we can stay in one conversation. More than that, we split into small groups of 3-5 that each work the themes and present their findings back to the room. Either way, we open by framing the question, not by presenting a solution, and we end by turning the discussion into a first set of evaluation criteria.

In Workshop 1 we discuss the following themes:

  1. Purpose and stakeholders

    Why should we build a shared infrastructure and protocol at all, and who are the primary stakeholders beyond Nordic Media Lab's members?

  2. Architecture and layering

    What does an ideal, modular infrastructure look like that matches our shared needs and standards, and where can the application layer and the data layer be separated? Should everyone have a data/ID wallet, and should you be able to switch data provider the way we “switch banks” today?

  3. Open and closed

    What can and should be open, what should be closed, and how encrypted should the closed parts be?

  4. Governance and ownership

    How should governance, including ownership, be composed around a shared infrastructure?

  5. Breadth across platform types

    How do we account for all types of social platforms, per the Rebuild taxonomy?

From the discussion we draft a set of evaluation criteria to support our ambitions. We close by brainstorming existing protocols to investigate further, including whether there are elements we will need to build ourselves.

Agenda (about 3 hours incl. breaks)

  1. Welcome and framing 15 min, moderator

    Why we are here, the scope, and how the two workshops connect. We open the question. We do not present a solution yet.

  2. Opening round 15 min, everyone

    Each person names what a shared infrastructure would need to do for their platform: one hope and one worry. Everyone speaks early.

  3. The themes 55 min, one group or split

    We work through the five themes, either as one conversation or split into small groups of 3-5 that each take the themes and capture their points. The moderator decides on the day, based on how many we are.

  4. Findings 15 min, groups or plenary

    If we split, each group presents its 2-3 main points to the room. In a single group, we gather the points together and carry them straight into the criteria.

  5. Break 10 min
  6. Draft evaluation criteria 30 min, moderator

    We turn the discussion into a first list of criteria that any solution should meet.

  7. Protocols to investigate 25 min, everyone

    We brainstorm existing protocols to look into, and note anything we may have to build ourselves.

  8. Close and next step 15 min, moderator

    We recap the criteria and set the frame for Workshop 2: a concrete proposal to pressure-test against them.

The work in Workshop 1 forms the basis for Workshop 2, where we go deeper into concrete protocols and present a proposal to test against these criteria. Workshop 2 is expected late September 2026, more information to follow.

Workshop 2

The proposal

Expected late September 2026.

Here we present a concrete proposal for the shared infrastructure and take the temperature on whether there is support to develop it further, and which concerns we need to resolve along the way. The proposal is measured against the needs and evaluation criteria we formulate in Workshop 1.

Before Workshop 2 we share a short piece of background reading that makes the case for the direction we propose. It goes out in good time beforehand.

Agenda (about 3 hours incl. breaks)

  1. Welcome 5 min, moderator
  2. Presentation 30 min, Bjarke Winding

    A concrete proposal for a shared framework for Nordic Media Lab, and the reasoning for the foundation it is built on. Including the question: why this direction over the alternatives, whether a purpose-built protocol or the larger existing networks?

  3. Working groups 30 min, moderator
    • What concerns you specifically about basing this on the proposed foundation?
    • What would make you hesitate to commit development time to this?
    • Does the proposed content model fit how your platform is structured?
  4. Synthesis 30 min, moderator

    Each group presents its 2-3 points.

  5. Mandate check 30 min, moderator

    Do we have a mandate to continue developing the proposal in the direction described, with the concerns and suggestions we have noted?

  6. Close 10 min, moderator
    • We have a mandate to continue, possibly with reservations.
      Bjarke Winding gathers suggestions, questions and reservations and works them into the next version of the proposal for the following workshops.
    • The group wants a more thorough clarification process before we go on.
      Who is responsible, what needs clarifying, and what is the timeframe?
    • The group does not want to proceed.
      The process stops here.

If there is a mandate to continue

Bjarke Winding works on the next version of the proposal, incorporating reservations, suggestions and ideas. If needed, he works in depth with individual members and their concrete proposals.

After that, 2-3 further workshops are held, working the concept through by the same recipe: presentation, working groups, synthesis and mandate check. When the concept is judged mature, a final workshop is held where it is adopted or rejected. If adopted, a plan follows for possible funding and onboarding across the platforms.
